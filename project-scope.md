# Project Scope — Boliviana Café Website

## The Concept

**Boliviana** — _"A taste of Bolivia"_ — is a café in the heart of Berlin uniting three established Bolivian brands under one roof:

- **Die Seele Boliviens** ("The Soul of Bolivia") — handmade Bolivian pastries: salteñas, cuñapés, pucakapas, and artisan cakes.
- **Caraya Coffee** — Bolivian specialty coffee, direct-trade with Bolivian farmers, mostly women-produced, single-origin.
- **Miskisimi** — Bolivian wines and spirits, grown at high Andean altitude, plus Singani, Bolivia's national spirit.

Tagline direction: _"One bite. One sip. One story."_

The website should carry this same spirit: warm, vibrant, proud of its heritage, and inviting — not just a menu, but an introduction to Bolivian culture for a Berlin audience that may be discovering it for the first time.

## Who We Are Serving

- Berlin locals and international residents curious about Bolivian food and drink culture.
- Existing fans of the three brands who already follow them individually on Instagram.
- Walk-in café visitors who want practical info fast: hours, menu, location.

## Project Goals

- Easy to navigate and welcoming for first-time visitors.
- Mobile-first — most visitors will check hours/location on their phone.
- Visually vibrant, reflecting the brand's colorful, handcrafted identity (illustrated leopard mascot, Andean textile patterns, bold pink/orange/navy/purple palette — to be refined in a separate design file).
- Fast, secure, and easy for the café team to keep updated (especially opening hours, events, and daily availability).
- Available in **German, Spanish, and English**.

## Brand Voice & Story Ideas

- Each partner has its own story worth telling briefly on the site, with a link out to their own website/Instagram for people who want to go deeper.
- Emphasize authenticity: direct trade with Bolivian coffee farmers, traditional family recipes, wines grown at extreme altitude, handmade daily.
- A recurring note worth surfacing somewhere (menu/about): products are handmade fresh daily, so not everything is always available — invite people to ask at the counter.
- Self-service model: order and pay at the counter, not table service — good to set expectations before someone visits.

## Pages & Content

### Landing Page

- Navigation bar, current opening hours, and a banner for closing days or announcements (e.g. holidays, sold-out items, special hours).
- Strong intro visual/tagline capturing "a taste of Bolivia."

### About Us

- The combined Boliviana story (why three brands came together).
- Short story for each partner — Die Seele Boliviens, Caraya Coffee, Miskisimi — each with a "learn more" link to their own site/socials.

### Menu

- Organized by category, mirroring the brochures:
  - **Food:** Salteñas (chicken, beef, pork, vegan), Cuñapés, Pucakapa, Artisan cakes.
  - **Coffee:** Espresso-based drinks, batch brew, hand filter, seasonal single-origin selection.
  - **Tea & Other:** Teas, matcha, iced drinks.
  - **Wine & Spirits:** Red wines, Singani, signature cocktails (Chuflay, Iced Té con Té).
  - Include dietary/allergen icons (egg, gluten, milk, vegan, nuts) as shown on the physical menus.
- A note that daily availability can vary since everything is handmade fresh.

### Contact

- How to reach the café directly, plus address and map (Winsstraße 17, 10405 Berlin).
- Contact options for each partner individually, for brand-specific inquiries.

### Gallery

- A visual showcase of products and atmosphere, drawing from the partners' active Instagram presence (@caraya_coffee, @die_seele_boliviens, @miskisimi_imports).

### Upcoming Events

- Recurring café events such as coffee cuppings, barista courses, and cultural celebrations (e.g. Día de Santa Cruz + spring party) — the kind of thing currently handwritten on the café window/chalkboard.
- Should be easy for the team to update as new events come up.
